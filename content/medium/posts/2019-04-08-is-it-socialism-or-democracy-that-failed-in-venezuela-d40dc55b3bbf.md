<figure><img alt="" src="https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/0*zTaPfy5U8sGwynsS.png" /></figure><p>Hugo Chavez has always been a hero in newspapers and political magazines growing up mostly due to the love for communism Kerala had. His death was covered for an entire week and felt like the world lost a leader that day. Newspapers used to be filled with columns on how Venezuela as a country is so successful and rich in culture, education, healthcare and just social benefits. Chavezs socialist montage build out of oil seemed like any communist dream.</p><p>Muduro was also in the rising stages of a hero then. Chavezs trusted person who helped him build Venezuela was being president. Today, the mess that Venezuela has gotten to be was no surprise to anyone who critiqued the model Chavez created. This was bound to come. The most interesting part though, is how the country is dealing with it. Multiple governments around the world have fallen in the past decade, most of them fell prey to the vicious cycle of radicalisation. It was the same everywhere: autocratic heads of state, failing economy and state sponsored violence. All three happened in Venezuela as well. The army being used to counter protest and Russian mercenaries protecting Muduro was all the same to the rest of the world where this happened. Yet, barring a few isolates instances of citizen violence, no radical group demanding independence or murder of the president exist in Venezuela. No international terrorist group has been able to set foot there as a massive player. It’s not like venuzuela does not have communal lines or terrorist organisations near them. It’s a very interesting thing Chavez left off, Education and Healthcare. Interestingly that’s where all failed socialist, communist and left revolution based countries succeed.</p><p>Venuzuela has an extremely high literacy rate and basic healthcare system. People believe in things getting better one day, they believe in the system of democracy or any system that would work for the country one day. People don’t mind rallying, protesting and even fasting to death of the streets. Even China for that instance just lacked in English education but their basic educational skill set is what is making them rise to where it is headed. Kerala and West Bengal also are benefactors of this educational liberalisation, don’t know how exactly how they’re going to use it. At the end even if communist socialist dreams fail but has succeeded in education and imparting rational to a population it’ll ensure that society will get out of it one day and in all probability succeed.
