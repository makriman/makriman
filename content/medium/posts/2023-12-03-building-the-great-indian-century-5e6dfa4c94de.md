<p>Today is my last day of being 23 years old ever again.</p><p>Over the last couple of days, weeks, and months, I have been thinking, what next? Every time I wander away into other options and think of potential new horizons to explore, it just feels right when thinking about starting another company. A job would be nice but not fulfilling. Regardless of how hard it gets, every day when doing a startup, the new beacon of hope and excitement when you wake up is the most fulfilling part of building your own startup. Weirdly, I find fulfillment in that :)</p><p>So, what next? Artificial Intelligence is going to radically improve and transform our lives in the next couple of decades. E/acc or effective acceleration is more important than ever now to ensure that we keep the momentum towards building the future of artificial intelligence going. The one thing everyone building AI systems needs is human intelligence and humans.</p><p>We are parallelly in the cusp of 🇮🇳/acc or Indic acceleration. The next decades are clearly set to be the Indian decades. With a population of over 1.4 billion people and 600 million smartphones, the human intelligence to build artificial intelligence should largely be from India. This can open economic opportunities for millions to move out of poverty in India. I want to build a company that makes it happen.</p><p>We are building it at greatindiancompany.com.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*-Xdq83-vip-HEhQVeBVBsA.jpeg" /><figcaption>🇮🇳/acc</figcaption></figure><p>Great Indian Company isn’t just a name; it’s my assertion to the world.
