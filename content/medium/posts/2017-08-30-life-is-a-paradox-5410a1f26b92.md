<p>Anything that follows this page is just a display of humanities efforts to not face the question to which this forms the answer, what is the question?</p><p>What is the purpose of Life?</p><p>Well there is none, if there was, humanity would not have achieved what it has. Humanity always found purpose in success it thought achieved its purpose. Well, is that purpose? No.</p><p>Humanities purpose is beyond human explanation. Humanity would never achieve its purpose. Well what is the purpose, Doesn’t this make, Life a Paradox?</p><p>What if there was a purpose to life?</p><p>Well it would then be a simulation of a rat race. Let’s assume the purpose for everyone is the same. Everyone believes their existence is complete at a point in time when this success is achieved. Everyone would do their bare minimum to achieve that. Now when Humanity doesn’t know what the end is, where this rat race ends, when the race ends, Humanity thrives to perform, outperform and achieve to satisfy the humanitarian brain and mind that it has achieved something, achieved its purpose. But is that purpose?<br> No again.</p><p>Also, not everyone can single handily make a billion dollar corporation, not everyone can win freedom for a million, billion people. So, what becomes the purpose (or the humanitarian substitute for the same) for the common man? The kid who grows up and dies unknown to the world, maybe after a 100 years of existence on this planet.</p><p>Well we all are innately capitalist aren’t we? We standardized life purposes, created societal constructs and structures for humans to follow. Capitalism was never created, it always existed it always been an evolution of human mind and thought.</p><p>Human thought always standardized. Humanity said “You find your soulmate and That’s purpose in your life” Make kids, continue your legacy you have achieved your purpose” But are these real purposes in life? No.</p><p><em>Originally published at </em><a href="http://www.makriman.me/life-is-a-paradox/"><em>www.makriman.me</em></a><em> on August 30, 2017.</em></p><img src="https://medium.com/_/stat?event=post.clientViewed&referrerSource=full_rss&postId=5410a1f26b92" width="1" height="1" alt="">
